##David Lennox Booth

#Edgware 30.1.1927  -  Moylegrove 27.7.1996.


Written in 1997.

This page is my personal in memoriam for David Lennox Booth.
 He was truly a gentle man and I am very glad to have met him.

Of his many sides there is one that I want to bring forward here. Ever since being
 a little boy he has always been very fond of railways: an enthusiasm we
 shared.


[![the Kodak wallet](/assets/img/david-lennox-booth/wallet.thumb.jpeg)](/assets/img/david-lennox-booth/wallet.jpeg)


David was not a man of words, so I will keep this short. When going through
 his old files with his daughter Marion, at the time my partner,
 we found a [Kodak wallet](#on-the-wallet.html) containing dozens of
 photographs young David must have
 taken when he was a boy ([footnote](#footnote) ).
 Photographs of trains, trains, trains.
</P>

<P>
One of the most striking things of David Lennox Booth was his pleasure in
 making people happy by sharing what he had, often going to great lengths,
 yet without showing off. Therefore, I felt it is in his spirit to put a
 selection of these photos on the net. They may not be perfect, but they may
 mean something to someone interested in 1930's steam railways.
</P>

<P>
So far for what I wrote one year ago. Given the many, warm reactions I have
 received these pictures have indeed struck a chord, and Marion has told
 me quite a few times that David would have been very pleased, if not a
 bit surprised. Therefore I have now added seven more photos, even though
 the quality of these varies. I hope you will enjoy them, as so many did
 with the first ten. 
</P>

## Footnote

<P>
Assuming these pictures come from one film (and they came from one wallet)
 they must have been taken between
 February 1939, when LNER 0491, seen in photo 5, was delivered, and October 1943,
 when Quicksilver lost its Garter Blue livery. Since there are no obvious signs of
 the war - no blinds, for instance - my best guess is 1939-1940, when David was twelve,
 thirteen maybe. He was a real trainspotter then, his cousin told me recently, although
 the area covered (Reading, Slough; Cambridge?) makes it still a bit unlikely,
 since his parents probably didn't take him that far from home. All
 comments are welcome!
</P>
